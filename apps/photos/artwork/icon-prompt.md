# Sunflower icon master

`PhotosIcon-master.png` was created with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-15. The bundled app icon is exported as an opaque 1024×1024 PNG by `scripts/generate_artwork.py`. The system supplies the outer icon mask. No historical screenshot or Apple icon file is bundled.

Prompt:

> Create one square 1024x1024 full-bleed app icon artwork inspired by the classic iOS 6 Photos sunflower icon. A realistic close-up photograph of ONE large golden yellow sunflower viewed from the front at a very slightly low angle, head centered at x=54%, y=51%, dark brown densely detailed seed disk, many natural tapered overlapping vibrant yellow petals reaching almost the image edges, a short green stem and a small natural leaf partly visible at the bottom. Clear rich blue summer sky in the background with very subtle hazy white clouds. Beautiful sunny directional light, crisp photographic textures, warm golden petals, tactile real flower, high saturation like 2012 app icon photography. Very restrained polished glassy highlight at the top for a classic skeuomorphic app icon, no modern flat vector illustration. Fill the entire square edge-to-edge. No border, no rounded outer corners, no words, no text, no logo, no watermark, no transparency. This will be installed as a native iPhone app icon; iOS will apply the outer mask.
